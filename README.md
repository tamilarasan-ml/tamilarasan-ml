## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a technology professional with **12+ years of experience** delivering enterprise software across **Banking, Hospitality, and Geo Engineering** domains.

Throughout my career, I've evolved from a **Test Consultant** to a **Scrum Master**, **Project Manager**, and **Release Manager**, gaining hands-on experience across engineering, Agile delivery, stakeholder management, and product strategy.

Today, I'm expanding that foundation into **Artificial Intelligence** by building AI-powered applications using **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Agentic AI**, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Python**, and **FastAPI**.

I enjoy bridging **enterprise delivery experience** with **modern AI technologies** to create intelligent, scalable, and business-focused digital solutions.

With 12+ years of experience across Product Management, Agile Delivery, Business Analysis, and Software Engineering, I'm currently focused on designing and building next-generation AI systems using modern AI frameworks.
